# Phase 4 Plan: Multi-Email Vendor Master & ATS Communication Engine

## Goal
Enable multi-email (comma-separated) support for staffing partners in Vendor Master Lite (`VARCHAR(MAX)`), display the Email ID column in the vendor list table (without touching the Excel export), and ensure both vendor email touchpoints in the ATS recruitment module dynamically fetch and dispatch emails to all vendor recipient addresses.

---

## Analysis of the 2 Vendor Email Dispatch Places in Recruitment

1. **Place 1: Job Requisition Intimation / Assignment**
   - **Trigger**: In Job Management (`job-management.component.ts`), HR allocates vendors or clicks "Notify Vendors" to dispatch the job mandate.
   - **Backend Route**: `POST /api/v1/AtsLifecycle/SendVendorIntimationEmails` -> `SendVendorIntimationEmailInternalAsync`.
   - **Database Procedure**: `dbo.usp_REC_GetVendorsForRequisitionMapping` provides the vendor list and email.
   - **Action Needed**:
     - In `dbo.usp_REC_GetVendorsForRequisitionMapping`, change `ISNULL(cd.email, '') AS email` to `COALESCE(NULLIF(cd.EmailID, ''), NULLIF(cd.email, ''), '') AS email`.
     - In `AtsLifecycleController.cs` (`SendVendorIntimationEmailInternalAsync`), split `v.Email` on `,` and `;` so every email address in the comma-separated list is added to the outgoing recipient list.

2. **Place 2: Offer Letter Dispatch**
   - **Trigger A (Batch)**: In Candidate Pipeline (`candidate-pipeline.component.ts`), HR selects candidates in Offer Letter stage and clicks "Send Offer Letters to Vendors".
     - **Backend Route**: `POST /api/v1/AtsLifecycle/SendVendorOfferLettersBatch`.
     - **Query**: `GetCandidateOfferDetailsForVendorBatchAsync` reads `COALESCE(NULLIF(cd.EmailID, ''), NULLIF(cd.email, '')) AS vendorEmail`.
     - `EmailService.SendEmailWithMultipleAttachmentsAsync` already splits `toEmail` on `,` and `;`, automatically dispatching to all vendor emails.
   - **Trigger B (Single)**: In `IssueOfferLetterBatch`, when individual offer letters are issued and vendor email exists.
     - **Database Procedure**: `dbo.usp_REC_GenerateOfferLetter`.
     - **Action Needed**: In `dbo.usp_REC_GenerateOfferLetter`, update `@VendorEmail` resolution to query `dbo.REC_Candidate_Details` (`WHERE IsVendor = 1 AND (pk_recId = @VendorId OR Vendor_Code = @VendorId)`), selecting `COALESCE(NULLIF(EmailID, ''), NULLIF(email, ''))`.
