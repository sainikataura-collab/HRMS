   - Add method `GetTaskStakeholderEmpIds(int taskId)` or capture recipient list returned by `usp_HR_Task_DispatchNotification`.

   - Inject `IHubContext<TaskHub> _taskHub`.
   - On `CreateTask` and `UpdateStatusAndRemarks`:
     - Fetch stakeholder recipient empIds for that task.
     - Broadcast `TaskNotificationReceived` event with payload `{ taskId, taskCode, subject, eventType, priority, status, actorName, timestamp }` to stakeholder user groups.
     - Broadcast `TaskBoardChanged` event to `"TaskWorkspaceBoard"` group.
